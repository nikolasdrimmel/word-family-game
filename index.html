Word Family Rush

English

Beat the clock. Build the family.

You get one word from a word family. Type every other member of that family — each one you nail adds time to the clock and points to your score. Run out of time and the round ends.

  • 1
    Read the seed word and the part-of-speech cues below it — e.g. noun, verb, adjective, adverb.
  • 2
    Type each related word and press Enter. Right answers give +time and +points (and build a combo).
  • 3
    When two words share a part of speech, a short meaning-hint appears so you know which one is wanted. Accents/case don’t matter.
